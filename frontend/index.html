I understand that IRDSS may first share a limited clinical referral summary with candidate hospitals so they can evaluate capacity. My identity, complete clinical details, and protected attachments will be made available only to the hospital selected to receive the referral, except where disclosure is otherwise required or permitted by law.

I authorize the referring facility and participating IRDSS facilities to collect, securely transmit, access, and use the information reasonably necessary to coordinate this referral and provide care. I understand that consent may be withdrawn before disclosure or processing where withdrawal is legally and operationally possible, without affecting processing already lawfully completed.
