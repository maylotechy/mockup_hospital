Features

Everything a hospital needs to coordinate care across facilities

Patient Registry

Keep each hospital's patient records organized and ready to reference the moment a referral is needed.

Smart Referral Routing

Submit a referral with severity and location, and get matched with accepting hospitals ranked by available beds.

Live Resource Inventory

Update bed counts, specialists on duty, and equipment availability so the network always sees current capacity.

Location-Aware Matching

Referrals factor in patient location so recommendations favor hospitals that are actually reachable.

Per-Hospital Access

Each facility signs in with its own credentials and API key, keeping records scoped to the right hospital.

Real-Time Status

Track referral status as it moves from submitted to accepted, so nothing falls through the cracks.

How It Works

From patient record to accepted referral in three steps

1

Sign in to your hospital

Staff log in with hospital credentials to view patient records and manage referrals.

2

Initiate a referral

Select a patient, set severity and location, and the system finds hospitals that can take the case.

3

Choose the best fit

Review accepting hospitals ranked by bed availability and confirm the destination.
