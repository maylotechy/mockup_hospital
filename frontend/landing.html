About the Project

The Intelligent Referral Decision Support System (IRDSS) is a digital healthcare initiative developed under the Mindanao Center for Disease Watch and Analytics (DiWA). The system aims to improve collaboration among healthcare facilities across the Mindanao region by providing a unified platform for electronic patient referrals and secure health information exchange.

The IRDSS enables participating HPCNs to coordinate patient transfers more efficiently through AI-powered referral selection.
